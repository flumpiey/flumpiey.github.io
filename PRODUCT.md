# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two primary audiences, with equal priority:

- Founders and businesses who decide whether to give Malva Dev a build or rescue project, then make contact.
- Recruiters and hiring managers who screen Dru Connold for a role, then download the CV or make contact.

## Product Purpose

A single-page portfolio for Dru Connold, Full-Stack Developer and founder of Malva Dev, in Randburg, Gauteng, South Africa. Success is a visitor who judges the fit and then makes contact or downloads the CV.

## Positioning

Project rescue: Dru takes unstable or failed builds to production quality. The current examples are EngagementFit and Exclusive Books.

## Operating Context

- Page sections: summary, core skills, experience, projects (with a detail view per project), education, certifications, achievements and interests.
- Contact routes: email, Telegram, LinkedIn, GitHub and CV download.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is a prerendered static site on GitHub Pages, with no server runtime.
- Email and Telegram links stay obfuscated against scrapers.
- Do not add metrics, testimonials or client claims beyond the current site copy and the CV.

## Brand Commitments

- Names: Dru Connold, Malva Dev.

## Evidence on Hand

- CV: `static/250000 Professional - Dru Connold - CV.pdf`.
- Project write-ups in `src/routes/+page.svelte`: EngagementFit, DANSA, Exclusive Books, FinnPont, SmartBiii, manager-mcp.
- Project images and the portrait in `static/`.
- Public source code: `https://github.com/flumpiey/manager-mcp`.
- The site copy contains no testimonials. Do not create any.

## Product Principles

1. Show project rescue first, with the problem, the work and the result.
2. Serve clients and recruiters equally.
3. State only claims that the site copy or the CV supports.
4. Make contact easy and keep contact details protected from scrapers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA is a required standard.
